Brand identity

Vimalife

A wellness brand built on movement and balance

Client
Vimalife
Role
Lead designer
Scope
Logo · Brand guidelines · Moodboard · Signage · Merchandise · Social

I created the Vimalife identity for a wellness brand, from the logo and brand guidelines to the moodboard, signage, merchandise and social media that bring it to life.

01   The idea

Elevation in
a single mark.

Vimalife is a wellness brand built around movement, balance and community. The symbol is a stylised V set inside a triangle, with clean interlocking lines that stand for elevation, movement and strength.

Paired with a widely spaced wordmark, the mark feels calm, modern and confident. The idea of pursuit in motion guided every decision that followed.

02   Creative direction

Grounded, aspirational,
community-driven.

A moodboard set the tone before the identity was finalised: earth tones, natural materials and calm, high-end spaces, lifted by warm highlights of energy.

03   Brand guidelines

A system that
holds together.

Logo versions, clear space, colourways, a six-colour palette and a two-typeface system, documented so the brand stays consistent as it grows.

04   Spaces and signage

Calm from
the street in.

Exterior signage, a blade sign, reception branding and an embossed statement wall show the identity working inside a space, with natural materials doing much of the talking.

05   Merchandise

Branded
with restraint.

Caps, bags, hoodies, mats and towels carry the symbol subtly, embroidered, debossed or screen-printed, so the merchandise feels as calm as the space.

06   Social and print

Community,
in the feed.

Instagram profile and class schedule designs, a carousel and a printed flyer, all built in the brand colours.
