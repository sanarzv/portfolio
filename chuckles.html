Brand identity

Chuckles

Pizza, Burgers and Happiness

Client
Chuckles
Year
2021
Role
Brand designer
Scope
Logo · Brand guidelines · Packaging · Social media

I created the Chuckles brand from the start: the logo, the brand guidelines, the initial packaging and the first social media posts.

The identity is built around one feeling, happiness. A smile sits at the centre of the logo, and a bright mango yellow paired with black gives the brand its cheerful, edgy look.

01   The idea

A smile in
the logo

The Chuckles identity starts with a smile, cleverly integrated into the letter H. Inspired by the brand’s playful personality, I paired a vibrant mango-yellow palette with bold black contrasts to create an identity that’s energetic, recognizable, and full of character.

I also developed brand guidelines to maintain consistency across typography, colour, and visual applications.

02   Packaging

Packaging
that smiles

From burger boxes and cups to pizza boxes, takeaway bags, and tissue packaging, I extended Chuckles’ playful identity across its initial packaging range.

The recurring smile symbol and bold colour palette bring everything together, making the brand recognizable beyond the restaurant.

03   The brand in use

On the table,
and on the team

I carried the visual identity across menus, in-store collateral, promotional vouchers, cards, and staff uniforms, creating a consistent brand experience across customer-facing and operational touchpoints.

04   Social media

Social that feels
like the brand

I developed the initial social media visuals and profile design, translating Chuckles’ playful identity into a cohesive digital presence.
