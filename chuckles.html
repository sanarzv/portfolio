Brand identity

Chuckles

Pizza, Burgers and Happiness

Client
Chuckles
Year
2021
Role
Brand designer
Scope
Logo · Brand guidelines · Packaging · Social media

I created the Chuckles brand from the start: the logo, the brand guidelines, the initial packaging and the first social media posts.

The identity is built around one feeling, happiness. A smile sits at the centre of the logo, and a bright mango yellow paired with black gives the brand its cheerful, edgy look.

01   The idea

A smile in
the logo.

The H in Chuckles becomes a smile. The palette pairs faux-ripe mango yellow, chosen for the warmth and appetite it brings, with black for a classy, edgy contrast. The guidelines set how the logo, colour and type work together so the brand stays consistent as it grows.

02   Packaging

Packaging
that smiles.

The initial packaging range, from burger boxes and cups to pizza boxes, bags and tissue, all built on the same symbol and palette.

03   The brand in use

On the table
and on the team.

The identity applied to menus, in-store pieces, vouchers, cards and uniforms.

04   Social media

Social that
feels like the brand.

The first social media posts and the profile, designed to follow the guidelines.
