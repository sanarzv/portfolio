Project overview

Project Aurora focused on positioning Westinghouse’s AP1000® reactor technology within Canada’s evolving nuclear energy landscape. I led the creative direction and developed digital campaign assets designed to communicate a complex technical story across multiple audiences, including utilities, supply-chain partners, and the wider public.

01   The challenge

The challenge

Translate complex nuclear technology messaging into clear, compelling campaign visuals that resonate with Canadian audiences while maintaining a cohesive visual identity across digital platforms.

02   The approach

Make the AP1000®
the hero

The creative direction centered on three key messages: proven technology, Canadian capability, and readiness. A cohesive visual language connected the campaign, while the messaging was tailored to each audience.

Supply chain

Partnership opportunities, qualification, and jobs.

Utilities

Delivery risk, schedule and cost predictability, and Canadian control.

Public

Reliable, affordable power and jobs.

03   The work

DOOH and
LinkedIn

I developed DOOH banners and LinkedIn programmatic ads, combining bold messaging, impactful imagery, and clear visual hierarchy to communicate the AP1000®’s key messages across different digital environments.
