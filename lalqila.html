Brand refresh

Lal Qila

A heritage restaurant brand, refreshed for every touchpoint

Client
Lal Qila
Year
2022
Role
Designer
Scope
Brand refresh · Menus · Loyalty cards · Print · Packaging

In 2022 I refreshed the Lal Qila brand across print, packaging and in-restaurant collateral, giving a well-loved restaurant one confident, regal look from the menu to the delivery bag.

01   The idea

Regal,
not heavy.

Lal Qila means Red Fort, so the refresh starts from the building itself. Deep crimson, arched shapes and a repeating border pattern give the brand a presence that feels rooted and proud.

The goal was one look that could sit on a fine dining menu, a pizza box and a delivery rider’s bag without losing its character. The wordmark stays at the centre every time.

The restaurant was marking 21 years of excellence, so the refresh needed to honour that history while feeling fresh for a new generation of guests.

02   Menus and print

Menus that feel
like an invitation.

Menus, brochures and in-restaurant print designed as one family, from the main menu to the cocktail list and buffet tags.

03   Loyalty and access

Cards with
a sense of status.

Tiered membership cards, gate passes and vouchers that make guests feel recognised the moment they hold them.

04   Billing and stationery

Even the bill
looks the part.

The smaller details, from the bill folder to the pens, carry the same border pattern and crimson, so the brand stays present through the end of the meal.

05   Packaging

Packaging, from
takeaway to gifting.

From pizza boxes and takeaway trays to cake bags, velvet gift pouches and retail products, every item a guest takes home still carries the brand.
